# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a good project:

- Giving and gracefully accepting direct technical feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project rather than for one contributor
- Showing empathy toward other people
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

Technical disagreement is not incivility. Arguing hard about a design decision is welcome;
arguing about the person making it is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take
appropriate corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private report through
[GitHub's reporting flow](https://github.com/kingsokafor777-droid/basalt-aws/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.
